RustDeskHop is a free, independent, open-source companion for RustDesk.
It is not affiliated with or endorsed by RustDesk.

This installer adds RustDeskHop for your current Windows account, creates a
Start Menu shortcut, and registers an uninstaller. Startup and desktop
shortcuts are optional. No administrator rights are requested.

Exit RustDeskHop from its tray menu before upgrading. Leave RustDesk running.
The installer does not install, configure, stop or uninstall RustDesk and
does not modify saved computers, network profiles or credentials.

RustDeskHop has no telemetry. Requested connections can probe your configured
server and launch RustDesk. See the installed PRIVACY.md for details.

Original project code is GPL-3.0-only. The complete license, third-party
notices and matching-source download information are included. There is no
purchase, subscription, or additional license agreement required.

Uninstall through Windows Installed apps. Settings and recovery backups are
retained deliberately so reinstalling does not lose your configuration.
